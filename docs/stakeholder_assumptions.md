# Stakeholder Assumptions & Governance Framework

## Key Assumptions

1. **Client Sovereignty**: Clients have absolute ownership over what categories of information may be shared with specific professional roles.
2. **Role-Scoped Access**: Counsellors and Social Workers receive only context relevant to their scope of care and authorized by client consent.
3. **Admin Restrictions**: System administrators manage system capacity, metrics, and audit logs but MUST NOT automatically receive unrestricted access to raw confidential session notes.
4. **Human Decision Support**: The continuity summary engine is a decision-support tool. Staff members remain responsible for final clinical approval of handovers.
5. **Synthetic Dataset Scope**: Synthetic data represents realistic workforce profiles across Work Mode and Language Group dimensions without containing real PII.
